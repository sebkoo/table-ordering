# NNNN. Title: the decision, not the topic

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true that forces a choice. Constraints, obligations, and the shape of
the problem. External sources belong here, cited well enough that a reader can
check them. Keep the observation and the inference apart: say what a source
reports, then say separately what this project concluded from it.

## Decision

What was chosen, in the present tense. One paragraph is usually enough.

## Rejected alternatives

At least one entry. Name the alternative, then the specific reason it lost. An
alternative with a strong case keeps its strong case here.

- **Alternative.** Why it was not taken.
- **Alternative.** Why it was not taken.

## Consequences

What follows, including what this makes harder. Optional when there is nothing
a reader would not already infer.
